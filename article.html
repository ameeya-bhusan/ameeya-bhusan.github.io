1. Inline and Display Equations

Inline math sits in the text, for example the energy $E = mc^2$ or a vector $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^n$. Display equations are centred on their own line:

$$ i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\,\Psi(\mathbf{r},t) = \left[-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2 + V(\mathbf{r},t)\right]\Psi(\mathbf{r},t) $$

Aligned multi-line derivations work as well:

$$ \begin{aligned} \hat{H} &= \sum_{pq} h_{pq}\, a_p^\dagger a_q + \frac{1}{2}\sum_{pqrs} g_{pqrs}\, a_p^\dagger a_q^\dagger a_s a_r \\ &= \hat{H}_1 + \hat{H}_2 . \end{aligned} $$
